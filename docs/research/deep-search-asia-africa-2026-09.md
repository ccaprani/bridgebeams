# Asia, Africa and Middle East deep search: named section leads

Checked 22 September 2026. [Structured records](data/deep-search-asia-africa-2026-09.json) contain nine **new source URLs** across seven countries. These are research leads, not nine implemented sections or nine approved standards. The exact names below are retained for searching and correspondence. The previously registered Q-girder sheet is discussed below for comparison but excluded from the new-record count.

## Highest-yield drawing leads

1. **Qatar: Ashghal Q-Girders, TY1–TY10 and TYE1–TYE10.** The [current QCS 2014 standard-detail index](https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/en/Pages/SDDDocuments2014.aspx) lists SD 5-1-101 Rev 1, SD 5-1-111 Rev 1 and SD 5-1-112 Rev 1 under Bridges. The [Q section sheet](https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/en/SDDDocumentsLibrary/SD%205-1-101%20Rev%201%20Q-Girder%20Sections.pdf), already in the PDF transcription registry, names Types 1–5 at depths 800, 1050, 1250, 1550 and 1900 mm. The [TY](https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/en/SDDDocumentsLibrary/SD%205-1-111%20Rev%201%20TY%20-%20Beams.pdf) and [TYE](https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/en/SDDDocumentsLibrary/SD%205-1-112%20Rev%201%20TYE%20-%20Beams.pdf) one-sheet drawings each tabulate ten identifiers and depths 400–850 mm in 50 mm steps, with section area and inertia. The [general notes, SD 5-1-100 Rev 2](https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/en/Pages/SDDDocuments2014.aspx), require the designer to develop each generic detail for its particular application. The TY and TYE sheets are the new immediate transcription targets; request clarification only for tiny chamfers or dimension strings that remain illegible after local high-resolution rendering. Ashghal lists contact centre **188** on its index.

2. **China: Beijing atlas 20BGQL2.** Beijing's [official atlas landing page](https://ghzrzyw.beijing.gov.cn/biaozhunguanli/bzgj/tytj/202105/t20210527_2399594.html) serves a [54-page drawing PDF](https://ghzrzyw.beijing.gov.cn/biaozhunguanli/bzgj/tytj/202105/P020210527325354365083.pdf): *时速100公里及以下城市轨道交通后张法预应力混凝土单线预制箱梁* (single-track post-tensioned precast box girder for urban rail at up to 100 km/h). Its code is **20BGQL2**. The design note on PDF p9/printed p1 states 25, 30 and 32 m span variants. PDF pp22–24/printed pp14–16 carry girder drawings and a parameter table; printed p14 dimensions a 1600 mm external depth for the shown section. The drawings, not the general design note, must establish whether all span variants share that outline. This is a Beijing urban-rail atlas, not a highway standard. The main road Chongqing T-girder atlas in the [earlier regional report](research-asia-africa.md) remains a separate existing lead.

3. **Indonesia: Waskita's own PC-I H-series and Semi-T.** Search indexing for the producer-hosted [2026 catalogue](https://waskitaprecast.co.id/uploads/pdf/waskita-katalog-produk-2026.pdf) suggests PC-I **H-90, H-125, H-140, H-160, H-170, H-185, H-210, H-230** and **Semi-T** on Product Dimension p14; these names and table values are **not directly verified** because the PDF returns HTTP 403. Its [PC-T product page](https://waskitaprecast.co.id/product/beton-precast/pc-t-girder) confirms another named bridge-girder product and supplies a manufacturer contact route. The dimension-variable schematic and values need direct visual verification before transcription. A designation such as PC-I is not proof of geometric identity with WIKA's existing PC-I series.

4. **Sri Lanka: RDA drawing T/B/505.** The [RDA tender drawing pack](https://rda.gov.lk/rda_project_new/uploads/procurement/50/attchment_172287482193214602_tender%20drawing%2018-3.pdf), PDF p10, drawing RDA/NWP/T/497-9, calls out **Drg No T/B/505** for 20 prestressed beams of 13,500 mm finished length and shows a small “SECTION OF BEAM.” A [second RDA tender pack](https://rda.gov.lk/rda_project_new/uploads/procurement/403/attchment_174176038339765831_Drawings%2019.pdf) confirms the same drawing number but distinguishes 13.42 m cast from 13.5 m finished length. The standalone T/B/505 sheet is the best request to the RDA; the North Western Province design office telephone **+94 37 222 8836** is printed in the first drawing's title block. The existing ICC/RDA drawing identifiers in the older register are different and cannot yet be equated to T/B/505.

5. **Japan: Bi-Prestressing System Association trial sections.** Its [プレキャスト桁の標準断面 page](https://bipre.sakura.ne.jp/cross/index.html) labels **バイプレ方式プレキャストI桁** (bi-prestressed precast I-girder) and **バイプレ方式プレキャスト中空桁** (bi-prestressed precast hollow girder). The page explicitly calls these trial designs for depth-constrained road bridges at spans 25–50 m in 5 m steps; section drawings are linked as GIF images. Contact is through the page's お問い合わせ link. These are distinct from the SMC producer's Japanese PC-girder catalog already registered.

## Academic and project trails

- **India — NHAI project PSC I-section.** The official [NH 45-A Package II structural drawing pack](https://nhai.gov.in/nhai/sites/default/files/2020/PKG_II_Modified_Structural_drawings.pdf) includes drawing **FIPL-HD-TPT-117-V-N-45A-MJB-CH-50+473-GA-01**, sheet 03/03, titled “General Arrangement Drawing for Major Bridge (9×35.0 m) at design Ch 50+473.” It labels a “Detailed for Precast PSC I Girder” mid/end section. This is a **final feasibility report**, not a construction-issue drawing. The 35 m number belongs to the span arrangement, not the girder depth. The PDF is large and the contour must be checked at high resolution before extraction; the title block identifies Feedback Infra as design consultant.
- **Indonesia — university case study.** [Irkham Nofiyanto's 2024 Universitas Islam Indonesia thesis](https://dspace.uii.ac.id/handle/123456789/50078) compares a **PCI H-2.1 m** section on the Solo Balapan–Kadipiro elevated railway with a **PCU H-1.85 m** study alternative for a 40 m span (repository abstract). The PCI is described as the project type; **do not count PCU as constructed**. The attached thesis must be read for the full profile and original PCI supplier. This is a second route to producer contacts, but it may overlap WIKA or another manufacturer until identified.
- **Ethiopia — university section-development trail.** [Beruk Berhane's 2002 Addis Ababa University thesis](https://etd.aau.edu.et/items/cc61a905-fcb6-4cc0-8e04-e1d9e4bdeb9f) says its study prepared PCI-based standard sections for different simply supported spans (repository abstract). **No exact designation or dimension is exposed by the abstract.** The attached thesis has not been visually checked; this remains a blocked academic lead, not a claim of adopted Ethiopian sections. The AAU School of Civil and Environmental Engineering is the contact route.

## Search vocabulary that worked

| Area | Native query terms to reuse | Productive filter |
|---|---|---|
| China | `预制箱梁 构造图`, `后张法 预应力 单线 预制箱梁`, `预制T梁 通用图` | Municipal planning/transport authority PDF libraries; distinguish 轨道交通 (rail) from 公路 (road) and 公示/征求意见 (consultation) from issued drawings. |
| Japan | `プレキャスト桁 標準断面`, `プレキャストI桁 断面図`, `中空桁 桁高` | Association/producer drawings, including images where text indexing misses dimensions. |
| Indonesia | `gelagar pracetak prategang penampang`, `PCI girder H-170`, `PCU girder`, `Katalog Produk girder` | Producer catalogues plus university theses to find project variant and supplier names. |
| Qatar/Arabic | `كمرات سابقة الصب مقاطع`, `عارضة TY`, `Q-Girder Sections` | Ashghal standard-detail library; drawing number `SD 5-1-101`, `111`, `112` is more reliable than English title alone. |
| India/Sri Lanka | `precast PSC girder section`, `T/B/505`, `I girder general arrangement drawing` | NHAI/RDA drawing title blocks; do not infer geometry from a bridge span or bills of quantities. |

Scope decisions: precast building beams, cast-in-situ box decks, steel girders and copied textbook exercises were excluded. An indexed dimension is recorded only when a linked primary source itself prints it. Where a PDF was not retrievable or its drawing has not been read at sufficient resolution, the JSON says exactly what remains missing.
