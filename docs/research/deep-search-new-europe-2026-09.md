# New Europe bridge-girder search, 22 September 2026

This bounded search found **two new, dimensioned Greek project-specific girder leads** in an official regional technical description and an NTUA thesis. They are *not* national section families or evidence that the particular sections were built. The [machine-readable records](data/deep-search-new-europe-2026-09.json) are source leads only; no geometry has been implemented.

## Ranked leads

1. **Greece — Dervenakia bridge Γ3, precast prestressed double-T/I girder.** The [Region of Peloponnese technical description](https://www.ppel.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/8_-%CE%A4%CE%95%CE%A7%CE%9D%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%97-%CE%A0%CE%95%CE%A1%CE%99%CE%93%CE%A1%CE%91%CE%A6%CE%97-%CE%94%CE%95%CE%A1%CE%92%CE%95%CE%9D%CE%91%CE%9A%CE%99%CE%91_final_signed-2.pdf), printed pp. 34–35 (PDF pp. 34–35), specifies six 39.0 m girders per span: 2.40 m deep, 1.40 m top flange, 0.90 m bottom flange, 0.30 m web at mid-height, and 1.50–2.00 m solid rectangular end zones. The cover names the Region's Directorate of Technical Works as a contact route. Seek the dimensioned bridge Γ3 design drawings to resolve flange thickness, haunches and longitudinal transitions. The document describes a proposed road project; build status was not checked.
2. **Greece — Strymonas-area study bridge, asymmetric I/double-T girder.** [Evgenia Eleftheriou's NTUA thesis](https://dspace.lib.ntua.gr/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/51220/diploma_thesis_eleftheriou_evgenia_2020.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), §2.3 and Figs. 8–9, printed/PDF pp. 18–19, contains a rendered, dimensioned midspan section: 2.05 m depth, 1.25 m top width, 0.90 m bottom width, 0.25 m web; seven 40.5 m variable-section precast prestressed beams serve each 42 m span. The thesis title page names NTUA, author and supervisor Ioannis Psycharis. The introductory text, printed p. 15, says the model uses site data from a real Strymonas-area bridge, not that the drawn girder was built. Request its source design drawing to confirm the varying support section and detailed contour.
