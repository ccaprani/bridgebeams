# Brazilian Portuguese beam sources

The Brazilian search found two strong academic section leads and one federal guidance source. The structured records are in [deep-search-brazil-2026-09.json](data/deep-search-brazil-2026-09.json).

The [UFC thesis](https://repositorio.ufc.br/bitstream/riufc/68766/1/2016_tcc_dlfilgueira.pdf) defines a prestressed precast I beam for highway decks, with the section drawing in Figures 8–10 and a study range up to 25 m. It is a study-specific profile until the dimensions can be transcribed and independent use is found.

The [IBRACON/SciELO paper](https://www.scielo.br/j/riem/a/k55Zx3qyqfFyNRVvLCf8ccB/abstract/?lang=pt) studies five precast prestressed U beams for a 33.5 m bridge under Brazilian NBR standards. Table 1 gives section area and inertia at transfer and operation, which makes it a useful validation target; the complete U outline and composite slab contribution still need separation.

The [DNIT IPR 751/2022 guidance](https://www.gov.br/dnit/pt-br/central-de-conteudos/atos-normativos/tipo/instrucao-normativa/2026/sei_24795123_anexo_vii.pdf) confirms that Brazilian federal-road practice uses precast prestressed I- and T-section longitudinal beams, typically for 10, 15 and 20 m spans. The cited bridge album is the next retrieval target for standard drawings.
