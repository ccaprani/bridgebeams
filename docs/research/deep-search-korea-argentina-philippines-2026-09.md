# Korean, Argentine and Philippine follow-up leads

This targeted pass found four useful records in [structured form](data/deep-search-korea-argentina-philippines-2026-09.json). They identify standardisation work or named products, but none has enough independently checked outline data for implementation.

The strongest new retrieval target is Korea’s CODIL archive. A 2005 Korea Expressway Corporation report is explicitly about optimum design and standardisation of PSC I-girder bridges. A separate CODIL drawing gives a 30 m PSC-I girder with 2,000 mm depth, 700 mm upper flange and 660 mm lower flange, but the web and haunch geometry must be read from the original sheet.

Argentina has a historical but unusually direct standards lead: the TRID record for the Seventh Argentinian Road and Traffic Congress describes a 1972 report that formed part of the Argentine draft standard, with four premoulded prestressed beam types and cross-section details. The abstract does not expose the drawings, so this remains a retrieval request.

The Philippine DPWH/EMB Laguna Lakeshore EIS states that AASHTO girders are commonly used, describes a 40 m Type VI option and a 45 m NU 2000 option, and says NU girders were newly introduced on Cebu-Cordova. This is useful adoption evidence and a route to NU geometry, but the host blocked direct retrieval of the figures. It does not change the existing rule: a Philippine Type VI shares a US profile only after its full outline is compared.
