# AASHTO-named bridge beams beyond the United States: follow-up queue

Search date: 22 September 2026. The seven records are in [`data/deep-search-aashto-global-2026-09.json`](data/deep-search-aashto-global-2026-09.json). These are source leads, not new polygon profiles. AASHTO LRFD design/load-code adoption alone was excluded. A local beam called "AASHTO Type IV" should share a profile ID with the US standard only after comparing the dimensioned outline, including flange/web transitions and end/midspan differences. The Philippine `IV-B` suffix especially demands this check.

The strongest immediate geometry task is the DPWH Type VI drawing. For rapid contact, Grupo Muratori lists `info@grupomuratori.com` and `+502 2213-4200`; the other records identify agency, project and drawing/item locator for a targeted request. No email was sent.

Searches of Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam, Kenya, Ghana, Nigeria and Ethiopia mostly surfaced references to AASHTO LRFD/HL-93 design or load standards rather than a named AASHTO beam profile. Those references are deliberately not used to infer US-section adoption. Mexico COMPRE and the already catalogued Philippines DPWH Type IV drawing were also excluded as duplicate leads.
