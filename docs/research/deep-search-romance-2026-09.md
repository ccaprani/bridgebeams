## Strongest next transcription and contact leads

1. **Portugal, ISEP thesis** — [Barbosa's 2014 repository record](https://recipp.ipp.pt/entities/publication/ac6171fd-3cd7-45f3-b281-dcc0ef88b40a) links a PDF whose indexed Figure 5.5 and Tables 5.1–5.2 (PDF p.83 / printed p.63) name six study sections: `Viga I - 60`, `I - 75`, `I - 100`, `Viga T - 60`, `T - 75`, `T - 100`. The indexed tables give cross-sectional areas, but the current PDF download timed out; those numbers and Figure 5.5 need direct rechecking before use. These are academic designs, not automatically a Portuguese manufacturer's catalogue products. The repository item identifies the author and advisors for enquiries.
2. **Spain, Geoquest/Tierra Armada** — Its [manufacturer page](https://www.geoquest-group.es/reinforced-earth/elementos-prefabricados/) distinguishes `Vigas TPP` (inverted T), `Vigas TPP de techo plano`, and three artesa ranges (`estrechas`, `medias`, `anchas`). The page prints the TPP top/lower head widths and a 0.75–1.85 m depth range; artesa lower widths are 1.85/2.60/3.20 m. The producer says mould thicknesses vary by project. The page has an expert contact form; request dimensional drawings for fixed mould settings before implementation.
3. **Mexico, COMPRE** — [Producer page](https://www.compre.com.mx/vigas-aashto/) names `Viga tipo I` through `VI`, with printed depths for III–VI (115/135/160/183 cm). Contact `compre@compre.com.mx` for manufacturer drawings and verify whether these are exactly the corresponding US AASHTO sections; if so, use one profile identity assigned to both jurisdictions, consistent with the site's jurisdiction rule.
4. **Italy, Basso** — [Fly Bridge catalogue](https://www.gruppobasso.it/costruzioni/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2015/02/Brochure-Co.Ge_.Ba_..pdf), PDF p.47 / printed p.46, names U, V and box (`cassone`) bridge beams with a 36 m span envelope. The PDF was fetched directly and its text checked. Ask for each mould's cross-section drawing; the catalogue spread does not supply fixed section dimensions.
5. **France, CIMbéton** — [T80 PRAD booklet](https://www.infociments.fr/sites/default/files/article/fichier/CT-T80.pdf), PDF p.7 / printed p.10, Figure 5, distinguishes rectangular, I, and I-with-`blochet` factory-precast pretensioned beams, and prints typical dimensional ranges. These are types and ranges, not fixed producer sections. The [SETRA/Cerema VIPP guide](https://piles.cerema.fr/IMG/pdf/vipp_ponts_a_poutres_prefabriquees_precontraintes_par_post-tension_guide_de_conception_1996_cle645d2a.pdf), PDF pp.54–56, Figures 51–53, adds post-tensioned I-like beam proportion guidance; do not turn either guide's ranges into catalogue profiles.

## Search terms that worked

The productive shifts were from `prestressed beam` to native manufacturer and document vocabulary: Spanish `vigas artesa`, `vigas doble T`, `trabes AASHTO`, `prefabricados puentes catálogo`; Portuguese `vigas pré-esforçadas`, `longarinas pré-tracionadas`, `tabuleiros de pontes pré-fabricadas`; French `poutres préfabriquées précontraintes`, `PRAD`, `VIPP`, `poutres à talon`; Italian `travi da ponte`, `travi a cassone`, `catalogo prefabbricati`. Adding `site:` filters for producer domains, university repositories, Cerema/SETRA, and publisher archives found sources missed by broad English searches. Broader queries about `vigas para pontes` produced many building-joist and crane-runway false positives; explicit `pontes rodoviárias` or `tabuleiros` reduced them.

## Geometry gate

No source in this batch was promoted to an implemented profile. Several give nominal heights or shape classes but omit web/flange/chamfer dimensions. The ISEP Figure 5.5 and the Geoquest mould catalogue are the best near-term targets for full outline recovery; COMPRE is the clearest contact-ready request for section sheets. The French guides and Argentine thesis are valuable for terminology and cross-checks, but their design ranges must remain separate from fixed geometry.
