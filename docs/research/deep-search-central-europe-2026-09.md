# Deep search: Central Europe and Dutch bridge-beam families (22 September 2026)

Twelve new source records cover five countries and name at least 29 product designations or selectable modules. They are **research leads**, not implemented beam profiles. The existing registry already has Slovak VPH, Finnish Parma, Czech ŽPSV, Hungarian Ferrobeton, Dutch Haitsma HIP/HKO, German DDR BT, Swedish Abetong and Austrian TU Wien sources; those were excluded. Haitsma HRP was also excluded because the existing `nl_hip` source explicitly covers *HRP- en HIP-brugliggers*. Product availability, a height range, and a schematic drawing are not proof of a complete reproducible section outline.

## Ranked follow-up list

1. **Pekabex/Mosty Gdańsk MG-T18–46 (PL).** The [manufacturer's 10-page catalogue](https://pekabex.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Katalog-mosty-1.pdf), printed pp. 3–4 and 8 (PDF pp. 3–4, 6), shows three section positions and a ten-variant height/length/mass table. The [authors' conference paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/376349825_Projekt_i_ocena_nowego_typu_strunobetonowych_belek_prefabrykowanych_MG-T_do_zastosowan_w_mostownictwie), §3/Fig. 2, adds design rationale. **Next:** digitize support/mid/span outlines and edge variants. Contacts printed on catalogue back page: info@pekabex.pl; biuro@mostygdansk.pl.
2. **Haitsma HKP (NL).** [HKP product sheet](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4390/folder-hkp-ligger.pdf), p. 1, gives 1480 mm standard width, 1500 mm working width and 18–68 m span. [Profile drawings](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4402/hkp-april-2009.pdf), pp. 1–9, label HKP-600 through HKP-1400. **Next:** digitize the external box and internal void for each height. Manufacturer contact: info@haitsma.nl.
3. **Haitsma HGKO (NL).** [Sheet](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4409/folder-hgko-ligger.pdf), p. 1, gives 990 mm width and 7–16 m span. [Drawings](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4399/hgko-hgr-april-2-2009.pdf), pp. 1–5, label HGKO-400/450/550/600 (also HKO/HGR variants; avoid duplicate HKO registry). **Next:** transcribe individual outlined profiles. Contact: info@haitsma.nl.
4. **KŠ PREFA KSP-NPP / KSP-NDPP / KSP-NDPO (CZ).** [Product page](https://ksprefa.cz/produkty/dopravni-stavby/mostni-nosniky/), respective headings: I-section NPP, solid T NDPP, lightened T NDPO. It prints web, total width and depth *ranges*, not fixed variants. **Next:** request standard production drawings and lightened web geometry from published quick contact Ing. Petr Mrzena, p.mrzena@ksprefa.cz.
5. **Doprastav DPS DP I/10 (SK).** [Manufacturer page](https://www.doprastav.sk/2/mostne-dodatocne-predpate-prefabrikovane-nosniky-dps-dp-i10-dlzok-24-42-m/), product heading and *Použitie*, lists 24–42 m girders; [Beton 3/2023 article](https://www.ebeton.cz/wp-content/uploads/2023-3-36.pdf), printed pp. 37–38 (PDF pp. 2–3), reports 2 m deep bridge-203 members up to 42 m assembled from three segments. **Next:** obtain exact I/10 section and joint drawings. Use Doprastav's [prefabrication enquiry form](https://www.doprastav.sk/mostne-prefabrikovane-nosniky/).
6. **PREFA Nový Bydžov MS-I (CZ).** [Manufacturer table](https://www.prefa-nb.cz/predpjate-mostni-nosniky-vsti-2000), *Charakteristiky nosníku MS-I*, lists named variants, lengths and depths; first row MS-I-9/0,4 prints 8.96 m × 0.4 m and 0.67 m width. It explicitly says MS-I replaces VSTI 2000; the shapes must not be assumed identical. **Next:** request the MS-I cross-section; contact michal.bilek@msilnice.cz.
7. **Haitsma HBM / HKV / HGKP / HMP (NL).** [HBM](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4408/folder-hbm-ligger-boogbrug.pdf) is a curved U-girder, [HKV](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4393/folder-hkv-ligger.pdf) has a variable bottom, [HGKP](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4389/folder-hgkp-ligger.pdf) is a curved box, and [HMP](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4392/folder-hmp-ligger.pdf) is a solid-profile complete deck. All sheets p. 1. HBM has a separate [graphic-heavy profile sheet](https://www.haitsma-beton.de/media/4395/hbm-april-2009.pdf). **Next:** prioritize HBM/HMP fixed variants; request project-specific drawings for variable/curved HKV/HGKP. Contact info@haitsma.nl.
8. **Doprastav DPS VP I/10 (SK).** [Manufacturer product page](https://www.doprastav.sk/2/mostne-predpate-prefabrikovane-nosniky-dps-vp-i10-12-21-m/), heading and *Použitie*, lists 12–21 m pretensioned bridge girders; [product index](https://www.doprastav.sk/20/prefabrikaty-pre-mostne-stavitelstvo/) separately lists 24–30 m VP I/10 members. **Next:** establish whether these are one shape series or distinct profiles; request fixed sections through the [manufacturer form](https://www.doprastav.sk/mostne-prefabrikovane-nosniky/).
9. **SSF VMP modular plate-beam system (DE).** [Brückenkolloquium 2026 proceedings](https://elibrary.narr.digital/xibrary/kbr-2026-1), pp. 69–71, §1.1/Table 1, gives four selectable 200/40 to 100/100 cm modules and prestressed longitudinal members. This is **not** the steel-composite VTR solution built at the Elisabethbrücke in the same paper. **Next:** get author-dimensioned VMP module drawing and evidence of actual use. Public author contact: [Andreas Danders, SSF Halle](https://www.ssf-ing.de/kontakt/).

The two journal/proceedings sources above and the MG-T author paper give useful geometry or design context, but neither a bridge-wide cross section nor a photographed erection establishes every beam boundary. The JSON records carry a specific `missing_information` field so these names cannot silently become implemented sections.
