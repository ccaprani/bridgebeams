How the counts work

One count per named, source-backed discrete geometry choice. Documented reconstructions are included; namespace aliases, arbitrary continuous inputs, incomplete templates and Korean extrapolations are excluded. Counts show available catalogue geometries, not certification or design suitability.

Australian Super-T has nine type/subtype combinations at the nominal default flange. NZ Super-T has three documented width/depth arrangements. Ireland TY/TYE includes its distinct beam-and-slab and solid-slab shapes. Belgian and Greek templates need user-selected flange thicknesses and contribute zero fixed profiles. Source records include partial, inaccessible and rejected leads; multiple family records can refer to the same publication. Legacy implementations can exist outside the two recent regional research registries.

The United Kingdom currently has no profiles in bridgebeams.uk. Irish producer sections are attributed to Ireland, even when used or sold in the UK.
