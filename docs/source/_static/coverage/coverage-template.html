How the counts work

Country counts include each named, source-backed geometry available in that jurisdiction. A shared producer beam appears in each applicable country, while the global distinct-profile total counts its geometry once. Documented reconstructions are included; arbitrary continuous inputs, incomplete templates and Korean extrapolations are excluded. Counts do not certify design suitability.

Australian Super-T has nine type/subtype combinations at the nominal default flange. NZ Super-T has three documented width/depth arrangements. Ireland TY/TYE includes its distinct beam-and-slab and solid-slab shapes. Belgian and Greek templates need user-selected flange thicknesses and contribute zero fixed profiles. Source records include partial, inaccessible and rejected leads; multiple family records can refer to the same publication.

Banagher's 175 profiles are assigned to both Ireland and the United Kingdom because the producer documents availability in both. Their IDs are shared, so the global distinct-profile count includes them once.
